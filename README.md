# Ex.No.-1.1-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM

To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool.

## REQUIREMENTS

  1. Autodesk fusion 360
  2. Windows 10
  3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 2 GB or more)
  4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 
  
## PROCEDURE

Step 1: Draw vertical line 65 mm above the center point and vertical line 50mm below the center point. draw concentric circles of diameter 66mm and 40mm.

![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/113594316/198206497-ca83d495-119b-45cd-b43d-8ca3ea7e9544.png)

step 2: From the distance of 65mm from the center point draw two concentric circle 20mm and 32mm of diameter. From the distance of 50mm from the center point draw two concentric circle 10mm and 20mm of diameter.

step 3: At the left and right centre points, draw two concentric circles Ø20 mm and 14mm . The centres are located 47 mm to the left and right of the vertical centre line and 26 mm below the origin. 

step 4: Draw two circles 128mm and 40 mm and make appropirate tangent with the other circles to get the perfect curve.
